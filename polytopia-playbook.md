## Mandatory turn method (user 2026-09-09) — NON-NEGOTIABLE

1. **Screenshot** the board (and tech/star UI if needed).
2. **Write a short analysis** against this playbook (even in the computerUse report):
   - Turn / score / ★ bank / ★ per turn

   - Enemy contact: where, what units, threat to capital

## Resources & recruiting (user coaching 2026-09-08)
- Harvest fish/fruit/animals ASAP once unlocked — same turn.
- Ports after fish; coastal cities → Port → boats.
- Prefer Riders / Archers / Catapults / Bombers / Knights over Warrior spam.
- Tech without using unlocked tiles as failed tech buy.

## Terrain-first tech
- Read map every turn: water→Fishing→Port→Sailing; forest→Forestry→lumber; mountains→Mining; crops→Farming.
- Buy tech only if usable this turn on ≥2–3 tiles OR needed for an active fight.

## Economy hard rules
- Never end turn with 20+ stars if recruit/build/tech spend exists.
- Workshop early; 2–3 villages by ~T10–12.
- Domination = destroy enemy tribe, not score.

## Giants
- 40 HP, Attack 5, Defence 4, Movement 1, no Dash.
- Kite with Archers; Catapults; ≥2 defenders on capital/last city vs Giant.
- Never chase Giant off last city.

## Capture / explore HARD
- Must **stand ON** village/city tile (≥1 turn). Enemy cities: enter → "ready to capture next turn" → Capture.
- Explore fog every turn; no orbiting capital.
- After T10 with 1 city: spare units only explore for villages.

## Naval Domination (M17 lesson)
- Scouting/fleet fights alone fail. By ~T20 must embark + **land ON enemy cities**.
- Soft with Bombers/Catapults then melee/Giant onto city tile.

## Recurring failures — STOP
1. Stars ≥ unit cost and city can train → TRAIN before End Turn.
2. Village in range → move **onto** it this turn.
3. 1 city after T10 → explore, don't pile on capital unless enemy adjacent.
4. End Turn with ≥20★ → illegal.

## Priority checklist each turn (after screenshot analysis)
1. Threatened empty city → occupy/recruit (capital ≥2 on contact).
2. Village in reach → stand ON it; else explore fog/ruins.
3. Harvest → build (mines/farms/lumber/ports) → recruit BEST unit → useful tech.
4. Water: boats for scout **and** landing path to enemy cities.
5. Soft with Catapults/Bombers → unit ON enemy city → Capture.
6. End Turn only when moves/recruits/builds done AND checklist legal.

## Web research (2026-09-09) — fold into Bardur M19
Sources: Polytopia Wiki Bardur/Strategy, Strategies, Giant, Catapult; Game On Reviews first-10-turns + domination; KosGames domination stars.
- Bardur is a Turn-0 tribe: hunt animals on T0, upgrade capital immediately. Second warrior explores the other direction.
- Opening: Hunting (start) → harvest animals same turn → Organization if fruit exists → Forestry (lumber huts / chop) → Archery → Mathematics (Catapults by ~T12–14).
- First 10 turns: economy + 2–3 villages, ~12★/turn, Workshop on first city upgrade. Explorer only if huge map; else Workshop / +★.
- Tech costs rise with city count — buy needed tech JUST before capturing a new village when possible.
- Catapults: longest range, no retaliation, no Dash. Protect with Defender/ZoC. Knights/Riders delete unprotected Catapults. Use them to siege cities then walk melee/Giant ON the tile.
- Giant: siege contested cities; city level 5+ spawns Giant on the city tile and kicks a sieging enemy off.
- Easy 1v1 Domination: destroy the tribe (stand ON enemy cities). Score/temples do not win. By ~T20 must have a landing/siege path onto their capital/cities.
- Recurring M18 fail: never stood ON an enemy city. This match that is the win condition.
